Delete Your Account

Wheel Life — telemetry, ride tracking, and community for electric unicycle riders. Published by Airik Strandberg.

This page explains how to delete your Wheel Life account and exactly what is removed when you do. You can delete your account yourself from inside the app, or ask us to do it for you.

Delete it yourself, in the app

Android Open Settings → Account → Delete account, then confirm.
iOS Open Community → Profile → Delete Account, then confirm.

Deletion happens immediately. You do not need to contact us first, and there is no waiting period.

Ask us to delete it

If you have already uninstalled the app, or cannot sign in, email wheellifeapp@gmail.com from the address your account uses, with the word Delete in the subject. We will delete the account and reply to confirm, normally within a few days.

What is deleted

Deleting your account immediately and permanently removes, from our database:

  • Your account and sign-in credentials.
  • Your rider profile — display name, city, avatar, and your wheels.
  • Every ride you have shared — the database row for each one, including any route it carried.
  • Your leaderboard entries, badges, achievements, and kudos.
  • Your territory claims.
  • Your club memberships (see below for a club you founded).
  • Group rides you created, and your attendance of any others.
  • Your direct messages, club messages, group-ride messages, and comments.
  • Your wheel-diagnostic samples and other diagnostic notes tied to your account.
  • Your notification settings and push token.
  • Any Strava connection, along with the link between the two accounts. If you signed in with Apple, we also ask Apple to revoke that sign-in.

The database rows above are removed immediately. The files those rows pointed to — your avatar, profile cover, route files, ride photos, raw wheel captures, and report screenshots — are removed from cloud storage shortly after deletion, with automatic retries. If you founded a club and it is removed along with your account (see below), its photos are removed the same way.

This is not reversible. We cannot restore a deleted account or its rides.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Rides on your phone stay on your phone. Deleting your account removes the database rows on our servers; the local ride history that powers History, statistics and exports is untouched. Uninstalling the app removes that.
  • A club you founded passes to another rider: its longest-standing admin, or, if it has no admin, its longest-standing member. If you were the club's last member, the club is removed along with your account.
  • Messages you sent to another rider are deleted, not kept. Unlike an email, a direct message is one record shared by both of you — deleting your account deletes it for both sides, so it will also disappear from the other rider's conversation.
  • Backups. Deleted data can persist in routine encrypted backups for up to 30 days, after which it is overwritten. We do not restore deleted accounts from backups.
  • Records we are required to keep — for example where we must retain something to comply with a legal obligation or to resolve a dispute — are kept only for as long as that obligation lasts.
