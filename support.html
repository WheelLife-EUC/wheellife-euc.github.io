Support

Wheel Life — telemetry, ride tracking, and community for electric unicycle riders.

Get in touch Email wheellifeapp@gmail.com. We read everything and aim to reply within a couple of days. Reports of abusive behaviour or objectionable content are handled first — typically within 24 hours.

When you write in about a bug, these details get to an answer much faster: your wheel brand and model, your iOS version, the app version (Settings → About), and what you expected to happen versus what did.

Common questions

My wheel won’t connect

Make sure the wheel is powered on and is not already connected to another app or phone — these wheels accept only one Bluetooth connection at a time. Force-quit any other EUC app, then pull to refresh in the device picker. If the wheel appears but shows no data, it may be a model whose protocol we haven’t confirmed yet; email us and we’ll take a look.

My ride says “Recording…” or hasn’t synced

A ride uploads once it has finished. If your wheel disconnected and the ride never stopped on its own, force-quit the app and reopen it — the ride is recovered and uploads immediately.

Some values look wrong for my wheel

Wheel manufacturers don’t publish their Bluetooth protocols, so support for each model is worked out from real captures. A few models are still unconfirmed. If a number looks wrong, email us your wheel model and what it should read — and leave Settings → Data & Diagnostics → Share anonymous wheel data on, which sends the anonymous sample that lets us fix it.

How do I report or block another rider?

Tap the “…” menu on any rider, shared ride, or group ride, or open their profile — both offer Report and Block. Blocking is immediate and mutual. You can also report someone to us directly by email.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Community → Profile → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, shared rides, and group rides. Rides stored on your phone stay on your phone.

Do I need an account?

No. Connecting to your wheel, the live dashboard, alarms, ride recording, History, and exports all work without one. An account is only needed for the community.

Safety

Wheel Life shows you what your wheel reports — it does not control your wheel and cannot make riding safe. Telemetry can be wrong, delayed, or missing, and alarms can fail. Ride within your limits, wear protective gear, and never rely on the app to decide whether it’s safe to ride.
